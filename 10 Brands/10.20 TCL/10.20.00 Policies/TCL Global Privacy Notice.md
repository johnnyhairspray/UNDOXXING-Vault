# TCL Global Privacy Notice

**Source:** https://www.tcl.com/global/en/privacy-notice
**Effective:** 2025-07-18
**Controller:** Shenzhen TCL New Technology Co., Ltd., China
**Note:** US-specific version also at https://www.tcl.com/us/en/terms-privacy (same effective date, same controller)

⚠️ **NATIONAL SECURITY FLAG:** TCL is Chinese-owned. Per the Texas AG complaint: "The complaints by Paxton's office argued data collected by Hisense and TCL could be subject to China's National Security Law, which can compel companies to share data with the government."

⚠️ **ACTIVE LITIGATION:** Texas AG Ken Paxton filed suit December 15, 2025 against TCL Technology Group Corporation under Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act for unlawful ACR collection and sale to data brokers. Temporary restraining orders obtained against other manufacturers in same action (Hisense, Samsung). TCL status: active case.

---

## Scope

Applies to "TCL Materials" including TCL and Alcatel branded devices (phones, tablets, TVs, watches, wearables, routers), TCL smart service applications (TCL Account, TCL Channel, TCL Home App, BrowserHere/T-Browser, TCL Live, TV Guard, MagiConnect, Smart Gallery, Reminder, Weather, Game Center, Gesture Control, Remote Diagnosis), and website interactions.

---

## ACR — Automated Content Recognition

**The highest-priority data type for UNDOXXING scoring.**

Per the Texas AG petition (primary source): TCL Smart TVs capture screen images every 500 milliseconds — 7,200 images per hour. TCL employs this tracking across all devices connected to the TCL Smart TV, including external inputs such as cable, satellite, gaming consoles, and other HDMI devices.

TCL's internal name for this system: **"Watchware"** (per TX AG complaint).

Per the EU-version privacy notice (most explicit policy language on ACR): "Our third-party advertising partners may use automatic content recognition (ACR) and other technologies to collect your TV viewing and usage information to create profiles of users and determine the personalized advertisements that your devices will receive. You may control whether to allow us to activate the data collection by ACR by following the method provided in the settings page of your Smart TV."

**Data destination:** Shared with demand-side platforms, ad networks, measurement companies, data brokers, and cross-device tracking companies (per TX AG complaint). TX AG: "TCL harvests this data, builds profiles of consumers' behavior, and sells it for profit."

**Default status:** ON. Opt-out via Settings → Privacy → User Agreements → opt out of User Experience Improvement Program.

**Consent claim:** TCL claims consent is obtained during setup. TX AG disputed: "The so-called 'consent' TCL claims is meaningless. Disclosures are hidden, vague, and misleading."

**Consumer Reports 2025 confirmation:** "On 2025 TCL sets, you'll see the company's Terms & Privacy agreements once you've set up Google TV. You have to accept the agreement, but you can opt out of participating in the User Experience Improvement Program."

## Voice Assistant Data

Per policy (US and Global versions, identical language): "The Smart TV products capture data of the Amazon Alexa or Google Action voice assistant function on your Smart TV products. This information includes a device description, your voice, Skill Token and Voice command. When you use the voice assistant function, your voice data will be processed by Google or Amazon. Your data will be directly transferred to Google or Amazon."

**Capture mode:** Remote-activated (press-to-talk) — NOT always-on.
**Note:** TCL logs metadata; actual voice processing is by Google/Amazon under their respective policies.

---

## Usage and Device Data

**Policy language (US version):** "data about your usage of system functions, applications and smart services, and device activation and registration details, such as activation time format, page and button clicks, page viewed and time spent on those pages, time and duration of service usage, name and duration of videos played, and user favorites"

Also collected: IP address, MAC address, Device-ID, Terminal ID, serial number, IMEI, IMSI, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth information, connected device information.

**No opt-out available for device identifiers.**

---

## Personal Information (Account)

Collected when creating a TCL account or using Google TV (Google account required for full Google TV functionality): name, alias, postal address, phone number, email address, user ID/password, demographic information.

**TCL account:** Optional. **Google account:** Required for Google TV smart features.

---

## Geolocation

IP-based city-level geolocation: always active, no opt-out.
Precise GPS/location: opt-in only, with consent.

---

## Data Sharing

Per US policy: "In the twelve months since this Privacy Policy took effect, for commercial purposes, we have sold or shared the following categories of Personal Data" — followed by categories including online identifiers and usage data.

**Confirmed sharing destinations:**
- TCL Group companies (global)
- Third-party advertising partners and demand-side platforms (ACR data)
- Ad networks and data brokers (ACR data — confirmed by TX AG)
- Google / Amazon (voice assistant data, processed directly)
- Internet service providers (per CCPA disclosure)
- Law enforcement / government (including potential China NSL compelled disclosure)

**TCL's claim:** "We do not sell or otherwise disclose Personal Data you have provided to us, except as described in this Privacy Notice." The US policy's own CCPA table then lists categories sold/shared for commercial purposes — the policy simultaneously claims not to sell while disclosing sales.

---

## Data Retention

**Policy language:** "TCL retains your Personal Data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, and it will be deleted after a reasonable time." No specific retention periods stated for any data category. Score at maximum retention tier.

---

## Data Transfers

Data may be stored and processed globally. EU notice specifies data centers in EU, United States, Singapore, and Japan. "Our corporate group entities located in China may have limited remote access to our data centers to provide necessary service." Data subject to laws of the country where processed — including China's National Security Law for data accessible to Chinese entities.

---

## User Rights

CCPA/CPRA rights for California residents. GDPR rights for EU residents. Right to access, correct, delete, and opt out of sale/sharing. Exercise via TCL's privacy request form.

## Opt-Out Mechanisms

| Data Type | Default | Opt-Out Available | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACR / User Experience Improvement Program | ON | Yes | Settings → Privacy → User Agreements → opt out |
| Advertising ID | ON | Yes (delete/reset) | Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete Advertising ID (Google TV feature) |
| Google TV layer | ON | Yes (nuclear, setup only) | Choose "Basic TV" at initial setup — factory reset required to change |
| Voice assistant | On when used | Yes | Settings → Device Preferences → Google Assistant → off |
| Diagnostic data | ON | Yes | Settings → Privacy → User Agreements → opt out of diagnostic data |
| Location (precise) | OFF | N/A (opt-in) | Per-app permission |
| Device identifiers | ON | No | Always active |
| IP geolocation | ON | No | Always active |

## Samba TV Partnership

TCL and Samba TV announced a research and development partnership January 5, 2023 to "build next generation of AI powered smart TVs." Samba TV is an ACR data company. This partnership is relevant context for understanding TCL's ACR data infrastructure — Samba TV has its own privacy issues (FTC scrutiny) separate from TCL's policy.

## Legal Actions

- **Texas AG December 15, 2025:** State of Texas v. TCL Technology Group Corporation. DTPA violations for unlawful ACR collection and data broker sales. Active case. Part of simultaneous action against Sony, Samsung, LG, Hisense, and TCL.
- **Vizio precedent:** FTC $2.2M settlement 2017 for same ACR practices. Established legal framework for ACR enforcement.
- Texas obtained temporary restraining orders against Hisense and Samsung in the same action. TCL TRO status not confirmed as of scoring date.

## UNDOXXING Scoring Notes

**Key scoring decisions for TCL Smart TV products:**

- **ACR (HIGH/30):** Default on, fingerprints every 500ms, sold to brokers, buried opt-out — score at full handling% with 0.75x opt-out multiplier
- **Voice (MEDIUM/20):** Remote-activated not always-on — apply 0.5x capture mode multiplier
- **Device identifiers (LOW/10):** No opt-out — 1.0x opt-out multiplier
- **Retention:** Vague throughout — score all categories at maximum retention tier (7.5%)
- **Chinese ownership:** Flag on all TCL products — national security risk layer beyond standard scoring
- **Google TV dual layer:** Both TCL policy and Google's policy apply simultaneously. TCL policy scored here; Google's policy applies separately to Google account and Google TV platform data
- **"Does not sell" contradiction:** TCL's own CCPA table discloses sales/sharing for commercial purposes in the same document that claims not to sell. Score based on documented practice, not stated claim.

**Applies to:** All TCL smart TV products. Verify per-model hardware differences (microphone always-on vs. remote-activated, camera presence) before scoring individual products.
